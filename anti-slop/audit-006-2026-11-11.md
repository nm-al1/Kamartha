# Payment form and cash report, AFTER audit

Direction: `design/kamartha-design-responsive.figcraft`; supplied responsive
payment and REPORT-01 reference images. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.
Inter and supplied neutral/orange palette preserve product identity. Grouped facts
separate invoice context, receipt entry, cash totals, and individual transactions.
Spacing separates operational steps rather than decorative sections. No new
logo, illustration, invented customer names, or decorative motion was introduced.

## Findings awaiting approval

1. **HIGH, R-25:** existing white text on primary #FD3D18 remains below 4.5:1
   for normal text, including Tampilkan and payment review/save. This is the
   pre-existing global issue in audit-004, not resolved by the local expense
   action text fix. No unapproved global palette replacement was applied.
2. **LOW, R-11/R-20:** report date filtering still uses two explicit inputs and
   Tampilkan instead of the compact FigCraft range selector. Small-screen totals
   stack vertically for legibility. Payment form context uses a fact table rather
   than copying a resident badge with mock data. These are partial fidelity,
   not full pixel-parity claims.
