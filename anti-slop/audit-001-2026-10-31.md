# Post-build UI audit: stage B

Mode: after, explicitly selected by the user. This report does not authorize
additional UI edits. No finding below was silently fixed after the audit.

Direction: `docs/design.md`, orange Kamartha identity, locally bundled Inter,
calm task-focused forms. Design read: boarding-house management for one manager,
ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1. Orange identifies actions; restrained page
structure prioritizes business records. Forms stay within 640 logical pixels;
spaced groups separate cutover, files, recap and final confirmation. Cards group
individual records, not decorative feature claims. No invented image/logo asset
was added. These are implementation reasons, not a new brand direction.

## Numbered findings

1. **MEDIUM, R-06 typography:** regular explanatory `Text` still inherits
   Material's 14px body-medium default, whereas `docs/design.md` specifies 16px
   body text and 14px metadata. Seen in preparation guidance at widths 360 and
   1280. Suggested fix: define the body text theme explicitly, keep metadata 14px,
   then rerun mobile/zoom checks. Await user approval of finding **1**.

## Checks and confidence

- R-03: screenshots at 360/390/430/1280 and room-form widget interactions showed
  no observed overflow. This is not proof for every populated/error/zoom state.
- R-24/R-26: listed destinations exist. Live browser clicks opened both master
  forms and Escape cancelled them; fake-repository widget tests exercised
  preparation save/edit/finalize navigation. No full mutating browser claim.
- R-27: master lists have empty/loading/error paths; preparation has loading/error,
  incomplete-draft and empty-installation states.
- R-32/R-35: Escape cancellation and scoped form interactions were exercised.
  Full keyboard traversal, screen-reader review and axe checks are not verified.
- R-17/R-18/R-36/R-38: no testimonials or invented KPI values were introduced;
  counts in the application come from API data. The active home page explicitly
  labels later-stage functionality as unavailable instead of faking a dashboard.
- R-25: no complete contrast audit was executed; do not claim universal WCAG AA.
- Visual regression: **inconclusive**, because no approved screenshot baseline exists.

Visual approval remains open on finding 1 and the unexecuted accessibility checks.
This is a post-build audit, not an unconditional visual-delivery PASS.
